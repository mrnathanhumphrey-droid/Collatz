## 1. The ambient Hilbert space

For each level n ≥ 1, the natural Hilbert space is

> H_n = L²((Z/3^n)*, π_n)

where π_n is the stationary distribution of the Syracuse Markov chain on (Z/3^n)* (Tao's Syrac(Z/3^n)). The Fourier coefficients μ̂_n(ξ) = Σ_r π_n(r) e^{-2πi r ξ / 3^n} for ξ ∈ (Z/3^n)* are the inner products ⟨δ_ξ, π_n⟩ in the dual picture.

In the profinite limit, the relevant Hilbert space is the inverse limit:

> H = lim←{L²((Z/3^n)*, π_n)}

equipped with the natural projective system of conditional expectations.

## 2. The per-step transfer operators T_j

From C1_TAO_RECURSION_FORM.md (eq. 7.2, Tao's Proposition 7.1 one-step peel form; project-internal formulation from R75/R77):

**Definition.** The per-step transfer operator T_j (j ≥ 1) acts on functions f : (Z/3^n)* → C by:

> (T_j f)(ξ) = Σ_{v ≥ 1} 2^{-v} · A_v^{(j)}(ξ) · f(ξ · 2^{-v} mod 3^n)

where the weight factor is A_v^{(j)}(ξ) = e^{-2πi ξ · 3^{2j-2} · 2^{-b_{[1,j]}} · (phase terms)/3^n}, incorporating:
- The geometric weight 2^{-v} from v_j ~ Geom(2)
- The phase factor at step j: χ(3^{2j-2} · 2^{-b_{[1,j]}}) from Tao's eq. (7.4)
- The level-shift bookkeeping factor from the Syracuse recursion (R75)

In Tao's pair-grouping form (C1, eq. 7.4–7.5), T_j corresponds to the conditional expectation factor:

> f(3^{2j-2} · 2^{-b_{[1,j]}}, b_j) = E(χ(x · (2^{a_{2j}} + 3)) | a_{2j-1} + a_{2j} = b_j)

with x = 3^{2j-2} · 2^{-b_{[1,j]}}.

**Crucial coupling.** The phase argument of T_j depends on b_{[1,j]} = v_1 + v_2 + ··· + v_j, the sum of all 2-adic valuations accumulated through step j. This is not a property of step j alone; it is a running sum that couples T_j to all prior steps T_1, ..., T_{j-1}.

## 3. The algebra A

**Definition.** Let A be the von Neumann algebra generated by the family {T_j : j ≥ 1} acting on H (or on the finite-level truncations H_n, with the appropriate inductive limit). In the finite-level approximation at level n:

> A_n = W*({T_j : 1 ≤ j ≤ ⌊n/2⌋}) ⊂ B(H_n)

where B(H_n) is the algebra of bounded operators on H_n.

**Properties of T_j established in the project (R77):**
- T_diag = (1/5)·[[1,1],[4,4]] on the (P_+, P_-) class-resolved subspace
- Eigenvalues of T_diag: {0, 1} on eigenvectors (1,-1) and (1,4)
- Off-diagonal correction Off_j decays at rate 1/2 per level

## 4. The subalgebra B (2-adic accumulators)

**Definition.** Let B be the von Neumann subalgebra generated by the multiplication operators {M_{b_{[1,j]}} : j ≥ 1}, where M_{b_{[1,j]}} denotes multiplication by the function b_{[1,j]}(ω) = v_1(ω) + ··· + v_j(ω) on the probability space Ω = (ℕ+1)^∞ of Geom(2) sequences.

Here b_{[1,j]} is the running 2-adic valuation sum:

> b_{[1,j]} : Ω → ℕ,   b_{[1,j]}(ω) = Σ_{i=1}^{j} v_i(ω)

These are random variables on the same probability space as the T_j operators.

**Embedding.** The operators T_j act on H (or H_n), while the b_{[1,j]} are multiplication operators on L²(Ω, μ_Geom). The natural ambient algebra A is taken over the tensor product space H ⊗ L²(Ω). In this ambient:

> B = W*({M_{b_{[1,j]}} ⊗ I_H : j ≥ 1}) ⊂ B(H ⊗ L²(Ω))

**Commutativity of B.** The operators b_{[1,j]} are real-valued functions on Ω, so M_{b_{[1,j]}} and M_{b_{[1,k]}} commute for all j, k. Hence B is **abelian**.

---

## 5. The conditional expectation φ : A → B

**Definition.** Let φ = E_B be the conditional expectation onto B with respect to the natural trace on A. In the Geom(2) model:

> φ(a) = E_{v_1, v_2, ...}[a | σ(b_{[1,j]} : j ≥ 1)]

the conditional expectation onto the σ-algebra generated by all running sums b_{[1,j]}.

**Note.** Since b_{[1,j]} = Σ_{i=1}^j v_i, the σ-algebra generated by {b_{[1,j]}: j ≥ 1} is **strictly coarser** than the full product σ-algebra σ(v_1, v_2, ...). Conditioning on {b_{[1,j]}: j ≥ 1} does not determine the individual v_i's; it determines only their partial sums. This has direct implications for Phase 3.

## 6. Well-definedness check

| Object | Status |
|---|---|
| H_n = L²((Z/3^n)*, π_n) | Well-defined; π_n is a bona-fide probability measure on a finite group |
| T_j as bounded operator | Well-defined for each fixed level n; the Geom(2) weights 2^{-v} are summable |
| A = W*({T_j}) | Well-defined as a von Neumann algebra on the appropriate Hilbert space |
| B = W*({M_{b_{[1,j]}}}) | Well-defined; abelian von Neumann algebra of real multiplication operators |
| φ : A → B | Well-defined as conditional expectation; B-bilinearity follows from the standard theory of conditional expectations in von Neumann algebras |

**No object is ill-defined for the Syracuse/Tao recursion setting.** The setup is well-posed.

---

## 7. Summary

The operator-valued probability space is:

> (A, φ, B) = (W*({T_j, M_{b_{[1,k]}} : j,k ≥ 1}), E_B, W*({M_{b_{[1,j]}} : j ≥ 1}))

where:
- A is the von Neumann algebra generated by all transfer operators and all accumulator operators
- B is the abelian subalgebra of accumulator operators (running 2-adic valuation sums)
- φ is the conditional expectation onto B

The key structural feature is that **T_j is not B-measurable**: it depends on b_{[1,j-1]} (accumulated before step j) through the phase factor, but it also introduces a new independent increment v_j. Thus T_j and B interact non-trivially, which is precisely what makes the B-freeness question non-trivial.
