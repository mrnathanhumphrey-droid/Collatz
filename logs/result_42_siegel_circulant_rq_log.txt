## VERDICT (Lead 2):
   Siegel's circulant DIAGONALIZES the SINGLE copy (eigenvalues = Fourier values F_k(n)).
   Parseval bridges to ||pi_k||^2 = q^{-k} sum|F_k(n)|^2 (= our Lean Parseval).
   BUT r_q = decay rate of the SECOND MOMENT sum|F_k(n)|^2 -- NOT a single eigenvalue
   (R28: no closed form). The second-moment operator IS build_M. So the circulant gives
   NO analytic shortcut to r_q; it NAMES r_q as the L^2 Fourier-decay rate of a Riesz-
   product / self-similar measure (Kahane-Salem-Zygmund territory). H_DECOUPLE: see rates.
