## Structural derivation (exact, not numerical)

The result is forced by the level-grading of `x_j(b_prior) = 3^{2j-2} · 2^{-b_prior} mod 3^n`:

- **j = 1**: `x_1(b_prior) = 2^{-b_prior} mod 3^n`. Since `2^{-b_prior}` is a unit, `x_1 ≡ ±1 mod 3` (never ≡ 0).
- **j ≥ 2**: `x_j(b_prior) = 3^{2j-2} · 2^{-b_prior}`. Since `3^{2j-2} | x_j` and `2j-2 ≥ 2`, we have `x_j ≡ 0 mod 9` (a fortiori `mod 3`).

The Kraus operator action is `M_v^{(j, b_prior)}(f)(ξ) = 2^{-v/2} · e^{-2πi ξ x_j · 2^{-v}/3^n} · f(ξ · 2^{-v} mod 3^n)`. Within a 3-fiber `{ξ_0, ξ_0 + 3^{n-1}, ξ_0 + 2·3^{n-1}}`, the phase factor at `ξ_0 + a·3^{n-1}` is
`e^{-2πi (ξ_0 + a·3^{n-1}) x_j · 2^{-v}/3^n} = e^{-2πi ξ_0 x_j · 2^{-v}/3^n} · e^{-2πi a · x_j · 2^{-v} / 3}`.

The inner factor depends only on `x_j · 2^{-v} mod 3`. Since `2^{-v} mod 3 ∈ {±1}` is a unit, this is `≡ 0 mod 3` iff `x_j ≡ 0 mod 3`. Therefore:

- **j ≥ 2**: phase constant within each 3-fiber → `σ_{-v}` permutes 3-fibers → both `D_W` and `D_class` preserved EXACTLY.
- **j = 1**: phase varies as `ω_3^{a·x_1·2^{-v}}` within fiber (non-trivial cube-root multiplier) → fiber-sums DESTROYED → `D_W` not preserved.

Furthermore, for j=1 the phase variation is precisely the third-root-of-unity twist that takes `D_W` onto its complement and vice-versa — explaining α = 1.0 exactly (maximally mixing, not just "leaking a bit").

The numerical ε ~ 10^{-15..-16} for j ≥ 2 is machine-precision floating-point error, not a structural artifact.

## Why dim(A'_{j ≥ 2}) is large (6 at n=2, 9 at n=3)

The j ≥ 2 family has restricted structure: at n=2 all j ≥ 2 operators reduce to bare shifts σ_{-v} (since x_j ≡ 0 mod 9 means x_phase = 0); at n=3 they take three distinct phase classes `x_phase ∈ {0, 9, 18}` mod 27. The full j ≥ 2 family is thus generated by:
- `σ_{-1}`: cyclic permutation of order ord_{3^n}(2) (= 6 at n=2, 18 at n=3),
- Phase operators in `C[ω_3^ξ]` (= identity at n=2, 3-element algebra `{1, φ_9, φ_{18}}` at n=3).

The joint commutant of (σ_{-1}, phase operators) is:
- **n=2**: phase trivial → A' = C[σ_{-1}] = abelian, dim 6.
- **n=3**: σ_{-1} swaps mod-3 classes; combined with mod-3 phase operators yields A' parameterized by `C[σ_{-1}²|_{class +}]`, a 9-dim abelian algebra. dim = 9.

In both cases dim(A'_{j ≥ 2}) ≫ 2 (block-diagonal), but the structure DOES contain `(P_W, P_{class})` as a coarser sub-decomposition.
