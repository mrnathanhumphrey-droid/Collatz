Connection: ||d_{k+1}||^2 to S_{k+1} - S_k
==============================================================================

  Algebra:
    sum_r' pi_{k+1}[r']^2 = sum over (r' mod 3^k) [alpha^2+beta^2+gamma^2]
    sum (a^2+b^2+c^2) = (a+b+c)^2/3 + (deviation-from-uniform terms)
    With a+b+c = pi_k[r']:
      = pi_k[r']^2/3 + (a-pi/3)^2 + (b-pi/3)^2 + (c-pi/3)^2

  So 3^(k+1) * sum_r' pi_{k+1}[r']^2 = 3^(k+1) * [sum_r' pi_k[r']^2/3 + ||d_{k+1}||^2]
                                    = 3^k * sum_r' pi_k[r']^2 + 3^(k+1) ||d_{k+1}||^2
  Therefore:
    Q_{k+1} = Q_k + 3^(k+1) * ||d_{k+1}||^2
