## 1. Higher (v, v') pairs reduce identically

From CROSS_FREQ_PHASE1_EXPANSION.md §4, the cross-frequency contribution Q_n^{ab}(c; v, v') depends only on g = v' - v (assuming v < v'). Therefore:

- (v=1, v'=3): g = 2, contributes to P^{−−} (both odd)
- (v=2, v'=4): g = 2, contributes to P^{++} (both even)
- (v=1, v'=5): g = 4, contributes to P^{−−}
- (v=3, v'=5): g = 2, contributes to P^{−−}
- (v=2, v'=6): g = 4, contributes to P^{++}
- (v=4, v'=6): g = 2, contributes to P^{++}
- (v=1, v'=7): g = 6, contributes to P^{−−}
- (v=3, v'=7): g = 4, contributes to P^{−−}
- (v=5, v'=7): g = 2, contributes to P^{−−}

**All pairs with same g contribute to the SAME cross-frequency moment M_n(g, c).** The weighting in the sum P_{n+1}^{++} = Σ_{v,v'} 2^{-v-v'}·Q_n is collected into W_+(g) = 2^{-g+1}/15 (geometric in g, ratio 1/4).

So the cross-frequency contribution is a *single* infinite sum over g:

  **Off_{n+1}^{++}(c) = (3/15) · Σ_{g=2,4,6,...} 2^{-g+1} · X̄_n(c; g) = (2/5) · Σ_{g even ≥ 2} 2^{-g} · X̄_n(c; g)**

  **Off_{n+1}^{−−}(c) = (2/5)·4 · Σ_{g even ≥ 2} 2^{-g} · X̄_n(c; g)** (factor 4 from W_−/W_+)

where X̄_n(c; g) = Σ_{a,b ∈ {+,-}} M_n^{ab}(g, c) (class-summed; equal to the M_n moment with TOTAL μ̂_n on both legs).

The (1, 4)-direction structure of Off survives across all g — at each g, the off-diagonal contribution is along (1, 4). The total Off is therefore a single point in the 2D plane scaled by the scalar Σ_g 2^{-g}·X̄_n(c; g), on the (1, 4) line.

## 2. The cascade question: does V_M close under Tao iteration?

We have:
  P_{n+1}^{++}(c) = T_diag part [closed on (P_+, P_−)] + Σ_g (weight) · X̄_n(c; g)

To make Off_lin a closed operator, we need the recursion of X̄_n(c; g) itself under n → n+1.

  X̄_{n+1}(c; g) = ?

This requires expanding M_{n+1}^{ab}(g, c) via Tao's recursion. Each μ̂_{n+1}^a(s) and μ̂_{n+1}^{b*}(s·2^{-g}) becomes a Σ_v Tao expansion at level n. The result is a doubly-nested cross-frequency object:

  X̄_{n+1}(c; g) = Σ_{v, v'} 2^{-v-v'} · Σ_{ξ ≡ c, twist by ẽ_g·2^{n+1}} · A_v(ξ) A_{v'}^*(ξ·2^{-g}) · μ̂_n(ξ·2^{-v}) μ̂_n^*(ξ·2^{-g}·2^{-v'})

— a "doubly-twisted" cross-frequency object at level n. After the same lift-fiber + unit-shuffle analysis, this reduces to twisted level-n moments parameterized by TWO g-like parameters.

**Heuristic claim (not derived here):** the cascade produces moments with g-parameters in the lattice {(g_1, g_2) ∈ {0, 2, 4, ...}²} or similar. The full V_M is generated by iterating Tao's recursion, and may close after finitely many iterations or generate an infinite tower.

Two scenarios:

(a) **The cascade closes at some finite truncation.** This would be a remarkable fact requiring proof. If it does close, T_V is a finite operator over Q and its spectrum is mechanically computable. The most natural finite truncation would be {g ∈ {0, 2}} (rank-3 operator) or {g ∈ {0, 2, 4}} (rank-5 operator), with closure errors absorbing into higher g.

(b) **The cascade is infinite.** Then T_V is on a countable-dim space; its spectrum requires analytic methods (transfer operator analysis, Mellin transforms, Banach space framework of Liverani / Nisoli applied to V_M with weighted norms).

The empirical rate-½ convergence through k=6 is consistent with either scenario but more naturally suggests (a) with effective rank 2 or 3 (since one rate dominates cleanly).

## 3. Structural prediction: the leading mode of T_V on V_M

If the dominant eigenmode of T_V has eigenvalue 1/2 (as conjectured), and lives mostly in the (g=0) slice (i.e., span{P_n^{ab}(c)}) with small (g ≥ 2) admixture, then:

- The (P_+, P_−) projection sees eigenvalue ≈ 1/2 cleanly.
- The (g ≥ 2) admixture is the "off-diagonal correction" Off_n empirically observed (T_lead_spectrum.md §2, ratios → 0.503).
- The T_V's slow eigenvector is approximately (1, 4, ε₂, ε₄, ...) where ε_g are small g ≥ 2 coefficients.

This matches the empirical structure of T_lead_2x2.py's numerical fit.

**To verify rigorously:** truncate V_M at g_max = 2 or g_max = 4, build T_V over Q, compute its eigenvalues. If one is 1/2 (or stably approaches 1/2 as g_max grows), the spectral framework is confirmed.

This work is the precise Phase 3b that would close the rate-1/2 question — but it requires deriving the recursion of M_n(g, c) → M_{n+1}(g, c), which is structurally similar in scale to the original T_diag derivation (and the v ≠ v' analysis here). Estimated additional cost: comparable to the entire T_diag + Phase 1–2 work, i.e., another multi-session probe.

## 4. Why a smaller-scale derivation isn't available

A natural shortcut would be: "express M_n(g, c) directly in terms of (P_+, P_−) values empirically computed at level n, hence reduce T_V to its (P_+, P_−)-image and read off 2x2 spectrum." This is exactly what T_lead_2x2.py does numerically.

The problem with that shortcut is the one CROSS_FREQ_PHASE1_SPAN.md §3 identified: **M_n(g, c) for g ≥ 2 is not a function of (P_+, P_−)_n**. It depends on the full π_n. Numerical extraction of T_V from (P_+, P_−)-data therefore FITS a 2x2 model that approximates the leading dynamics — but the fitted matrix entries are NOT Q-derivable, because the actual operator on V_M has non-trivial g ≥ 2 components.

The exact-Q answer requires computing M_n(g, c) directly from π_n at each level (which `cross_freq_compute.py` partially does, at n = 2, 3) and building the operator on the full V_M^{(g_max)} truncation.

## 5. Mixed-parity contributions to P^{+−} and structural-collapse preservation

R76 §11's structural collapse states P_n^{+−}(c) = 0 for n ≥ 2. Phase 1 §3 showed this is preserved by Tao's recursion:

- Off_{n+1}^{+−}(c) involves bilinears μ̂_{n+1}^+(ξ)·μ̂_{n+1}^{−*}(ξ), so one leg has v even (≥ 2) and the other has v' odd (≥ 1). The pair (v even, v' odd) has g = v' - v odd. From Phase 1 §3, **v_3(d_{v,v'}) = 0 ⇒ lift-fiber sum kills the contribution**.

So Off_{n+1}^{+−}(c) gets no contribution from cross-parity pairs. Combined with the diagonal v = v' case being absent (v can't be both even and odd), P_{n+1}^{+−} receives ZERO from Tao's recursion off-diagonal terms.

But wait — what about diagonal v = v' where one of the legs is +-class and the other is −-class? This requires v even on one leg AND v odd on the other, which is impossible. So P^{+−}'s diagonal is also zero.

**Hence P^{+−}(c) = 0 IS preserved by Tao's recursion in BOTH diagonal and off-diagonal components, automatically.** This is a structural identity, not an empirical fact.

This recovers the R76 §11 structural collapse from the Tao-recursion side, and confirms that the Off_lin closure on V_M restricted to "same-parity" (P^{++}, P^{−−}) is consistent with the (P_+, P_−) 2D reduction.
