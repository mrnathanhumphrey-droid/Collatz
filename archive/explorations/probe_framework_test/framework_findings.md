## Verdict

**Phase 3 (moderate growth)** is trivially confirmed: (Z/3^k)\* is cyclic, generators {2, 2^(-1)} give diameter n_k/2 with linear growth d=1. So DS-C 1994's moderate-growth precondition holds.

**Phase 2 (product structure)** is the discriminating test. Summary across k=5,6,7:

| k | |F_n| CoV (n) median | phase std (n) median | r0 disagreement median |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 2.842e-01 | 7.898e-01 | 5.555e-02 |
| 6 | 2.864e-01 | 9.412e-01 | 7.345e-02 |
| 7 | 2.747e-01 | 7.485e-01 | 6.816e-02 |

**Outcome B — DG/CDG framework applies with modification.** Per-character ratios F_n vary with n (CoV 2.842e-01) and across r0 (disagreement 6.816e-02) at moderate magnitude — within an order, suggesting a trajectory-averaged or path-dependent modification of the iid framework would close the gap.

### Why this outcome (structural reading)

DG/CDG product structure Q̂_n(χ) = F(χ)^n requires characters χ to be (approximate) eigenvectors of K. Equivalent: K must commute with the group action (shift-invariance). K_k = (3X+1)·2^(-V) has both the deterministic +1 and the deterministic ×3 — the +1 breaks shift invariance on (Z/3^k)\*; the ×3 shifts character index. 

From Probe 3 (Ayyer-Singla test): the multiplicative-only operator B_k: x → x·2^(-V) DOES have characters as eigenvectors with eigenvalues on |λ - 2/3|=1/3. K_k with the +1 destroys this, leaving the essentially-non-diagonalizable Jordan-tower structure (cond(V) ~ 10^14 → 10^17 across k=5..7).

### Synthesis

DG product structure is a property of shift-invariant (commutative-with-group-action) Markov kernels. K_k is not shift-invariant. The framework's character-Fourier diagonalization fails for the same structural reason K_k is not diagonalizable (Outcome E from Probe 3): the operator's natural function-space structure is the Jordan generalized-eigenspace tower, not the L²-character basis.

This does not mean DS-C 1994 sharp rates are unreachable — but they would require the *correct* basis (generalized eigenvectors / Jordan chains, or the inverse-limit transfer-operator basis on Z₃) rather than the character basis. Probe_profinite already showed ρ_slow ≈ 0.83 IS the inverse-limit convergence rate; that's where the rate lives, not in any character-Fourier factor of K_k.
