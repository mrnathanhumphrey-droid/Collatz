# FAURE candidate D — Faure-Tsujii 2021 "Micro-local analysis + band structure of contact Anosov"

**Source:** Faure-Tsujii 2021.

## Verbatim (key statement)

Refined version of Faure-Tsujii 2013 (candidate C) with micro-local treatment. Same hypotheses on contact Anosov flow X on smooth compact manifold M, smooth potential V ∈ C^∞(M). Band structure of Ruelle spectrum further refined; the unstable foliation's regularity (Hölder, not C^∞) is handled via micro-local techniques.

## Identification

Same categorical requirements as candidate C: contact Anosov flow, smooth compact manifold, smooth potential. The micro-local refinement does NOT relax the smooth-manifold hypothesis; it addresses the Hölder regularity of the (un)stable foliation INSIDE the smooth manifold setting.

## Match to Syracuse

Same NO_FIT as candidate C. Syracuse is profinite-discrete, not smooth-manifold continuous-time. The micro-local refinement does not help: there's no Hölder foliation to micro-localize, because there's no smooth manifold to begin with.
