## The crux (one line): sign-bearing vs magnitude-only
Two kinds of results exist. **Sign-bearing** (pins Re of the value / a specific coefficient with its sign) — rare, needs an explicit product formula or a limiting-distribution theorem. **Magnitude-only** (`|Σe(·)|` or `|μ̂|→0`) — the entire modern exponential-sum + Fourier-decay corpus; yields `|⟨cos⟩|≤discrepancy`, which is **consistent with ⟨cos⟩ >0, <0, or =0** — useless for a sign. **Almost everything applicable is magnitude-only.**

## Closest thing to a sign statement — and it's a warning
**Untrau's hypocycloid equidistribution** is the only result pinning the *distribution of the real part* (not `|·|`). Ported to our setting its message is: the natural limiting object is **origin-symmetric (mean Re ≈ 0)**, so positivity of ⟨cos⟩ must come from a **small, structured bias surviving on a symmetric bulk** — exactly our measured **~0.8% modulation ripple on a white spectrum** (MODREG), the kind of second-order sign that equidistribution-to-leading-order washes out and no magnitude theorem can see.
