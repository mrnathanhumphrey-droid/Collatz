# Inter-level renormalization operator R̃_k spectrum

Inter-level operator R̃_k = L_k · K_{k+1}^m · P_{k+1→k} on the level-k coprime state space. Tests whether R̃_k has a complex-conjugate eigenvalue pair near magnitude 0.984 (slow-mode rate from empirical eps_k two-mode fit).

## Convention note

The brief said only 2 of 3 preimages are coprime to 3 in Z/3^{k+1}. This is incorrect for k ≥ 1: for r ∈ Z/3^k with r mod 3 ≠ 0, the 3 preimages r, r+3^k, r+2·3^k all have r mod 3 = r mod 3 ≠ 0 (since 3^k ≡ 0 mod 3). So all 3 fiber members are coprime, and the uniform lift uses weight 1/3 each (not 1/2 over 2 members).

## Step 1: lift identities and projection identity

| k | n_k | n_{k+1} | max|π_{k+1} @ P - π_k| | max|L_A @ π_k - π_{k+1}| | max|L_B @ π_k - π_{k+1}| |
|---|-----|---------|----------------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| 4 | 54 | 162 | 2.78e-17 | 1.89e-02 | 6.94e-18 |
| 5 | 162 | 486 | 2.78e-17 | 9.46e-03 | 8.67e-19 |
| 6 | 486 | 1458 | 2.78e-17 | 4.72e-03 | 1.73e-18 |
| 7 | 1458 | 4374 | 1.39e-17 | 2.36e-03 | 8.67e-19 |

Projection identity (P @ π_{k+1} = π_k) holds to machine precision: dynamics descends cleanly through mod-3^k.

Uniform lift (L_A @ π_k vs π_{k+1}) generally **does not** match: π_{k+1} is not fiber-uniform. Document the size of this discrepancy below.

Conditional lift (L_B @ π_k vs π_{k+1}) matches to machine precision by construction (it's defined as π_{k+1}(r̃)/π_k(r) weights on each fiber).

## Verdict

**Scope note:** the brief's prediction (ρ ≈ 0.984, period ≈ 9.2) was the
two-mode fit logged at the time of the probe. That model has since been
falsified at k=11 and is in STATE.md Tier 5 (ruled out / walked back). The
current best empirical fit is an order-3 linear recurrence on ε_2..ε_11
with ρ_slow ≈ 0.827 (real). This probe therefore tests an already-walked-back
hypothesis; the negative result here is consistent with that walk-back.

**The 0.984 prediction does not appear in this R̃ construction.** Under
either lift (uniform A or conditional-from-stationary B) and either K_{k+1}
power (m=1 or m=2), the leading non-trivial eigenvalue of R̃_k = L · K^m · P
is at magnitude 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻³ for k=4..7. Nothing close to 0.984 (or to
0.827).

For both lifts the construction reduces algebraically to K_k on the level-k
space:

- Uniform lift A: P · L_A = I; L_A · K_{k+1} · P fiber-aggregates to K_k
  exactly (modulo M_{k+1} > M_k tail, below float-64 epsilon at the tested k).
- Conditional lift B: L_B reweights by π_{k+1}/π_k, but the level-k
  projection of P · K_{k+1} still recovers K_k to small corrections.

So this construction's spectrum reproduces K_k's spectrum, which lives at
|λ_2| ~ 10⁻³. R̃ as defined here is not the operator that generates the
slow mode — neither the (now-falsified) ρ=0.984 nor the (current best)
ρ_slow ≈ 0.827 from the order-3 recurrence.

Three places the actual slow mode might live, none pursued here:

1. A non-square deviation operator R: level-k → level-{k+1} with the
   slow mode showing up in singular values restricted to deviations from
   stationary.
2. A different renormalization map entirely — directly on the ε_k sequence
   rather than on π_k → π_{k+1}.
3. The order-3 recurrence ρ_slow ≈ 0.827 may itself be transient k-dependent
   structure, not a fixed eigenvalue.

## Side finding: rate-1/2 in the uniform-lift discrepancy at k=4..7

Under uniform lift, ‖L_A · π_k − π_{k+1}‖_∞ approximately halves per
k-step at the tested k:

| k | max |L_A · π_k − π_{k+1}| | ratio to prev |
|---|-----------------------------|----------------|
| 4 | 1.892e-02                   | —              |
| 5 | 9.455e-03                   | 0.4998         |
| 6 | 4.720e-03                   | 0.4992         |
| 7 | 2.360e-03                   | 0.5000         |

Three ratios within 0.0008 of 1/2. Characterized as: at the tested k=4..7,
the lift-discrepancy metric decays at rate ~1/2 per k-step.

**Caveat.** This is three data points across k=4..7. Three observations:

- An analytic derivation that this rate is structurally 1/2 has not been
  produced; the pattern could be finite-k transient.
- STATE.md Tier 5 flags rate-1/2 envelope on |ε_n|·2^n as falsified at k=7
  (where the previously-stable envelope-near-0.04 broke). The k=4..7 regime
  here is precisely the regime where rate-1/2 looked correct for ε_n before
