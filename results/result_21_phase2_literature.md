## ★ Our object is Konyagin §1.2, verbatim

> *"Exponential sums with exponential functions. Let `g ∈ Z_p`, `ord_p(g) = t`. Consider `S(a,g) = Σ_{k=0}^{t−1} e(a·g^k/p)`. Let `G` be the group generated by `g`. Hence `S(a,g) = S(a,G)`."*

Set `g = 2`, `t = ord_p(2) = d`, `G = ⟨2⟩ = H`. That is **exactly our subgroup and exactly the index set of our weights** `w(h) ∝ 2^{−ind(h)}`. And our incidence count (R13/R18: `a + j·s·b ∈ H`, `a,b ∈ H`) is his additive-energy family:

> **Theorem 2.1.** `N_2(b)` = #`{x_1+x_2 ≡ b : x_i ∈ G}`; under a size condition, **`N_2(b) ≤ 4|G|^{2/3}`**.
> **Theorem 2.3.** `|S(a,G)| ≤ (p·T_k(G)·T_l(G))^{1/(2kl)}·t^{1−1/k−1/l}` via additive energies `T_k(G)`.

So the reduction is correctly aimed: `cross(2)` is a genuine subgroup-incidence sum with standard machinery attached.

## ⚠️ But the machinery is on the WRONG AXIS

1. **Bounds are asymptotic in `q` and need large subgroups.** Theorem 2.1 carries an explicit size condition (`|G| < ((p−1)/(p^{1/4}−1))+1`-type); `N_2(b) ≤ 4|G|^{2/3}` is only nontrivial when `|G|` is large. **Our `d` can be tiny — `d = 2` at q=3**, the critical case. At `|H| = 2` every bound in the paper is vacuous, and `H = {1,2}` is a hand computation anyway.
2. **No `k` anywhere.** Konyagin bounds sums at fixed `q`. Our target is **k-uniformity at fixed q**. His machinery does not have our variable.

## ★★ Consequence — Phase 3 relocates (and sharpens)

The `k`-dependence is **not in the subgroup sum**. It is in the **cascade** (R20): a level-k collision requires `k` successive residue events, and R20 **measured** the per-level conditional rate at **≈ 1/q, stabilizing** (`L2 ≈ L3 ≈ L4`). So `c_k ~ (1/q)^{k−1}` × (per-level incidence mass), and the decay that makes `cross(k)` converge for q≥5 (and diverge at q=3, where... note `1/q = 1/3` is NOT 1, so the divergence is NOT simply this rate — see caveat) comes from the cascade product.

**Division of labour for Phase 3:**
- **per-level incidence mass** → Konyagin-type equidistribution (his tools, his axis: fixed q)
- **k-fold decay** → the cascade product (our structure, R19/R20; no literature exists)

> **Revised Phase-3 target: prove the per-level conditional probability is ≤ r_q < 1 uniformly in the level, with r_q < 1 for q≥5.**

Much sharper than "bound the character sum," and it splits cleanly: literature handles the per-level piece, k-uniformity is ours.

**⚠️ CAVEAT flagged for the post-compaction agent:** R20's per-level rate ≈ 1/q would give `c_k ~ (1/q)^{k−1}` at EVERY q including q=3 (1/3), which would CONVERGE — but q=3 DIVERGES (R15/R16, slope 7/15). So the per-level rate is NOT the whole story: the decay is a rate × a per-level MASS, and the mass must grow at q=3 to cancel the 1/q decay. R16's `3/q` framing (which failed as a raw rate) and this 1/q rate are two different measured quantities; reconciling them is unfinished. **Do not assume c_k ~ (1/q)^{k−1} — it is contradicted at q=3.** The honest open statement is: `c_k = (per-level rate)^{k−1} × (weighted incidence mass per level)`, and BOTH factors' q- and k-dependence must be pinned before Phase 3 closes.
