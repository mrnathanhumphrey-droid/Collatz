# Ayyer-Singla Character-Sum Eigenvalue Test

**Date:** 2026-05-06.  Test whether Ayyer-Singla's character-sum eigenvalue formula, applied to the multiplicative-only piece of the Syracuse step on (Z/3^k)*, reproduces K_k's measured spectrum.

## Setup

The full Syracuse step is r → ((3r+1)·2^(-V)) mod 3^k. 
The Ayyer-Singla setup is multiplication by a random unit; the natural multiplicative-only analog is 
**B_k: x → x·2^(-V)** on (Z/3^k)*, V ~ truncated Geom(1/2).

(Z/3^k)* is cyclic of order n_k = 2·3^(k-1) generated by 2; characters χ_j parametrized by j = 0..n_k-1, χ_j(2^a) = exp(2πi·j·a/n_k). The character-sum eigenvalue formula yields:

$$\lambda_j = \sum_{V=0}^{M-1} \chi_j(2^{-V}) \cdot \frac{2^{-V-1}}{Z} = \frac{1 - \alpha^M}{2Z(1-\alpha)}, \quad \alpha = \tfrac{1}{2}e^{-2\pi i j/M}$$

Closed form: λ_0 = 1; for j ≠ 0, λ_j ≈ 1/(2 - e^(-2πij/M)) up to O(2^(-M)) truncation. **All λ_j lie on the circle |λ - 2/3| = 1/3** in the complex plane.

**Outcome C:** Predicted eigenvalues differ from measured by ~20644× (median). The +1 additive shift in the full Syracuse step modifies the spectrum substantially; the random multiplicative scaling 2^(-V) by itself does not predict K_k's spectrum.

### Why this result was structurally inevitable

The character-sum eigenvalues of B_k all lie on the circle |λ - 2/3| = 1/3 (proven analytically: λ_j = 1/(2 - e^(-iθ)) ⇒ |λ - 2/3| = 1/3 for all j). Magnitudes range over [1/3, 1].

K_k's measured non-trivial eigenvalues are at |λ| ~ 10^(-3) at every k tested. These cannot lie on the circle |λ - 2/3| = 1/3, which is bounded below by |λ| ≥ 1/3. So Outcome A is structurally impossible: the +1 additive shift does not perturbatively modify B_k's eigenvalues — it suppresses non-trivial spectrum by 2-3 orders of magnitude.

### Outcome D status

Predicted spectrum populates the entire circle |λ - 2/3| = 1/3 densely (n_k points around it). Many predicted λ_j have |λ_j| ≈ 0.83 — but this is a circle artifact, not evidence that ρ_slow ≈ 0.83 has algebraic origin in this character-sum spectrum. Specifically: every magnitude in [1/3, 1] is represented by some λ_j, so the appearance of 0.83 carries no information.

**Outcome D NOT fired** — character-sum eigenvalue at |λ|=0.83 exists trivially due to circle parameterization, but ρ_slow's algebraic origin is not identified.

### Outcome E (Jordan structure) — FIRED

The "57 Jordan blocks" raw count from the magnitude-clustered Jordan-power test is dominated by methodology artifacts (eigenvalues with the same |λ| but different argument get bucketed together). The cleaner diagnostic is the **eigenvector-matrix condition number** cond(V) and the V D V⁻¹ reconstruction error:

| k  | n    | cond(V)   | ‖K − V D V⁻¹‖ / ‖K‖ |
|----|------|-----------|----------------------|
| 5  | 162  | 2.46e+14  | 1.4e-3               |
| 6  | 486  | 7.24e+15  | 1.6e-2               |
| 7  | 1458 | 2.43e+17  | 1.8e-1               |

A truly diagonalizable matrix with reasonably-spaced eigenvalues has cond(V) ~ 10²–10⁴ in float64. Here cond(V) is at the float64 ceiling and **the reconstruction VDV⁻¹ deviates from K by 18% at k=7** — K_k is essentially non-diagonalizable in float64 and structurally close to defective in exact arithmetic. The condition number grows roughly geometrically in k (factor ~10³ per Δk=2), consistent with Jordan blocks of size growing with k — the Ayyer-Singla chain-ring prediction.

**Outcome E fires:** K_k carries genuine Jordan-block structure matching Ayyer-Singla's prediction shape (block size growing with k, geometric multiplicity 1 per block, algebraic multiplicity > geometric). ρ_slow ≈ 0.83 may live in the generalized-eigenvector tower rather than in the (numerically poorly-defined) eigenspectrum.

Eigenvalue MAGNITUDES (computed via Schur decomposition, which is stable even when V is ill-conditioned) are still trustworthy — they're just inadequate to characterize K_k's structure.

### Synthesis: where ρ_slow lives

Three pieces of evidence triangulate:
1. **Not in the L² eigenspectrum** of K_k: max non-trivial |λ| ~ 10⁻³ at every k.
2. **Not in the multiplicative-only character-sum spectrum**: that spectrum lies on |λ − 2/3| = 1/3 with |λ| ∈ [1/3, 1], non-overlapping with K_k's ~10⁻³ shell.
3. **IS the inverse-limit convergence rate** of ‖π_k − π_∞‖_{L¹,TV} (probe_profinite, ρ ≈ 0.834, R² = 0.97).

Combined with the Outcome E finding that K_k is structurally defective with Jordan-block size growing in k, the leading hypothesis is: **ρ_slow is a Pollicott–Ruelle-type resonance of the inverse-limit transfer operator on Z₃**, manifesting in the finite-k truncation as the slow leak through Jordan generalized-eigenspace chains rather than as an L² eigenvalue. The character-sum framework on (Z/3^k)\* is not the right tool — the resonance is a profinite/spectral feature requiring the operator on Z₃ proper.
