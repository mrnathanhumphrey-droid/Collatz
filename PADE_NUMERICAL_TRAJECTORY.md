### Key observation: monotone-toward-z=2 pattern broken at [3/3]

R77.6's reading was "diagonal [n/n] sequence monotonically tightens toward z=2" based on [1/1] = 0.076 → [2/2] = 0.051. This was used as the Padé fingerprint of a branch-cut at z=2. The probe is now contradicted at [3/3]:

[1/1] → [2/2] → [3/3]: distances 0.076 → 0.070 → 0.081.

The [2/2]→[3/3] step is OUTWARD (further from z=2), not INWARD. The monotone-tightening signature R77.6 invoked is no longer present once ε_7 (the first numerical entry) is included.

This breaks H_Z2_STILL_DOMINANT cleanly **for the diagonal sequence**. The closest-pole IS still near z=2 at [3/3], but it is no longer cleanly converging to z=2.

### Convergence rate change at [3/3] (the first one with numerical ε_7)

The [1/1] → [2/2] convergence ratio was (0.070 − 0)/(0.076 − 0) = 0.92 (very slow, branch-cut-like). At [3/3], the distance INCREASES — convergence "rate" is now negative.

This is the structural signature of: **the leading singularity is shifting away from z=2 as more data is added.** Either (a) the true leading singularity is elsewhere, OR (b) higher-order branch structure at z=2 dominates at this order. The data does not yet distinguish.

### Off-axis primary poles appearing at [3/3]

[3/3] has a complex-conjugate pair at z ≈ -0.027 ± 0.179 i. This is the FIRST off-axis structure in any diagonal Padé. (R77.6's off-axis poles only appeared in the all-pole [0/4] approximant, which is well-known to be artifactual.)

## Disposition this analysis can support

Based on the hand-computed [1/1]..[3/3] + the Hadamard estimate at n=10..13:

- **H_SLOW_MODE_DOMINATES (z ≈ 1.016)** is NOT supported. The Hadamard radius at n=13 is 1.57, not 1.016. The pre-asymptotic at n=13 still has the singularity at |z| ≈ 1.5..1.7, not 1.016.

- **H_Z2_STILL_DOMINANT** is WEAKENED. The [3/3] diagonal broke the monotone-tightening pattern. The Hadamard estimate at n=10..13 has radius decreasing toward ~1.57. z=2 is not the dominant singularity at large n.

The hand [3/3] complex pair at |z|=0.18 is artifact (too far from the Hadamard radius), but [4/4]'s complex pair would be in the |z| ≈ 1.5 range and could be structural.

- **H_NUMERICAL_TOO_NOISY** is testable in Phase 5. Currently the data agreement across methods (power iter / scipy.eigs / FFT) at 1e-15 suggests precision is NOT the bottleneck.

**Pre-final disposition (modulo script confirmation of [4/4]/[5/5] pole locations):** H_TWO_SINGULARITIES_VISIBLE or its refinement H_COMPLEX_PATTERN. Single-singularity-at-z=2 is REFUTED by Hadamard at n=13.
