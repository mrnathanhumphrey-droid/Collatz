**Date:** 2026-05-14. Phase 3 of Cébron probe — profinite portability check.

## Question

For each load-bearing component of Cébron's framework, identify the **mathematical structure of GL_N(C) (or smooth Lie groups in general) that the proof actively uses**, and mark whether the analog for (Z/3^n)* (or the inverse limit Z_3*) is:

- **TRANSFER_CLEAN:** works for profinite without modification
- **TRANSFER_TECHNICAL:** profinite version is a technical extension (days to weeks of work)
- **TRANSFER_RESEARCH:** profinite version is research (months/years)
- **TRANSFER_BLOCKED:** structural obstruction — no profinite analog of comparable strength is known

---

## Component-by-component

### C-1. W*-probability space (§1.1, §2.1)

> "Throughout this paper, (A, τ) will denote a W*-probability space, two random variables A, B ∈ A will be said to be free if the von Neumann algebras generated by A and B are free." (Cébron p. 2)

**Smooth-Lie use:** None directly — this is abstract operator-algebra setup.

**Profinite analog:** L²((Z/3^n)*) with the (finite-dim) trace τ_n(T) = (1/|G|) Tr(T) is a perfectly good W*-probability space (in fact, a finite-dim factor). The inverse limit gives an L²(Z_3*) as a Hilbert space with a natural trace.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_CLEAN.** The abstract W*-probability setup is base-agnostic.

---

### C-2. The algebra C{X_i : i ∈ I} (§1.1)

> "C{X_i : i ∈ I} is the free algebra generated by I indeterminates (X_i)_{i ∈ I} and an indeterminate center-valued expectation tr." (Cébron p. 5)

**Smooth-Lie use:** None — this is a purely algebraic construction (universal property over Z).

**Profinite analog:** Same algebra; the only difference is what (A, τ) you evaluate it in.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_CLEAN.**

---

### C-3. Free cumulants κ and free log-cumulants L_κ (§2.4–§2.6)

The free cumulants κ are defined combinatorially via the lattice of non-crossing partitions NC(n). The free log-cumulants L_κ are defined via the **graded Hopf algebra Y(k)** (Cébron §2.5) with comultiplication ∆ and multiplication ·.

**Smooth-Lie use:** NONE in the *definition* — these are combinatorial objects. But the **identities** (e.g. the Hopf-algebra cohomology that defines L_κ from κ) hold for arbitrary W*-probability random variables.

**Profinite analog:** The combinatorial structure is base-agnostic. For an operator T on L²((Z/3^n)*), the free log-cumulants L_κ(T, …, T) are well-defined finite-dim quantities — they would just be **computable from the moments tr(T^k)** of the operator.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_CLEAN.** Free cumulants are combinatorial.

---

### C-4. Theorem 2.13 — multiplicative composition formula

> "τ(P(AB)|B) = (e^{D_A} P)(B)" for A, B free in (A, τ). (Cébron Thm 2.13, p. 24)

**Smooth-Lie use:** NONE — the proof (Cébron pp. 24-25 via Lemma 2.15 and the Hopf-algebra Y(k)) is purely combinatorial-algebraic, using only the lattice NC(n) and the free-independence factorization of joint moments.

**Profinite analog:** The theorem statement is base-agnostic — it would hold for any W*-probability space. **What it requires is freeness of A and B**, which is a property of the *specific* random variables, not of the base group.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_CLEAN at the algebra level.** But the **load-bearing freeness hypothesis** fails for Syracuse (R77 cross-frequency coupling). This is the same issue as in §C-7 below.

---

### C-5. Brownian motion on the group (§3.3, §3.5, §4.1, §4.2)

The construction of (G_t)_{t ≥ 0} as the solution of dG_t = dZ_t G_t (§3.5, eq 3.10) uses **free stochastic calculus** with the free Brownian motion Z_t (a process in (A, τ), constructed via free creation/annihilation operators on full Fock space).

The classical-finite-N construction (§4.2) uses the **Laplacian Δ_{GL_N(C)}** on the smooth manifold GL_N(C) with right-invariant metric ⟨X, Y⟩_{gl_N(C)} = N ℜ Tr(X* Y).

**Smooth-Lie use:** CRITICAL. The Laplacian Δ_{GL_N(C)} = Σ Z_a² (Cébron p. 45) is a sum of squares of **right-invariant smooth vector fields**. Itô's formula in §3.3.2 uses the **smooth differential calculus** on GL_N(C).

**Profinite analog:** (Z/3^n)* is a finite discrete group of order 2·3^{n-1}; there is **no smooth structure**. The closest analogs:
- **Random walks on discrete groups** (heat kernel on Cayley graph) — base-clean but the analog of a Brownian motion is a continuous-time random walk.
- **p-adic Brownian motion via Vladimirov operator** (Kozyrev 2007, Khrennikov-Shelkovich 2006) — exists in the p-adic literature but is **structurally different** from a Lie-group Brownian motion. The Vladimirov operator is a non-local PDO on Q_p, NOT a sum-of-squares Laplacian.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_RESEARCH.** The "free profinite Brownian motion" object would need to be constructed from scratch. The combinatorial recursion (Cébron's eq 3.15) is a smooth-calculus identity, NOT an algebraic one. The natural analog in the p-adic literature (Vladimirov) is conceptually distant from Cébron's free-circular construction.

### C-6. Itô calculus identities (eq 3.9, eq 3.15, Lemma 4.3)

Cébron eq 3.15:
> "d(G_1 ⋯ G_k) = Σ_{i=1}^k G_1 ⋯ dG_i ⋯ G_k + Σ_{1≤i<j≤k} δ(i,j) ε(i) ε(j) · G_1 ⋯ Ĝ_i ⋯ Ĝ_j ⋯ G_k tr(G_i ⋯ G_j) dt." (p. 35)

**Smooth-Lie use:** This is **Itô's formula** for products of stochastic integrals against (Z_t). It uses the **second-order differential calculus** of the smooth Lie group.

**Profinite analog:** None directly — discrete groups have no Itô calculus in the smooth sense. The closest is **martingale-difference identities for random walks on Z_p**, but these are not naturally expressed as a sum-of-squares plus a quadratic-variation term.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_RESEARCH.** A profinite Itô-analog would require constructing a free profinite Brownian motion first (per C-5), then deriving the analog of eq 3.15.

---

### C-7. Freeness in the Syracuse setting

Cébron Theorem 2.13 requires A and B to be **free** in (A, τ).

**Smooth-Lie use:** None directly — freeness is an algebraic property.

**Syracuse status:** The single-step operators T_j and T_{j+1} in Tao's renewal product (after pair-grouping, eq 7.5) share the 2-adic exponent b_{[1, j]}, which appears in both. They are **explicitly NOT free** — the dependence is direct.

This is **the same obstruction identified in v1**: R77's cross-frequency v ≠ v' bilinear coupling **is** a measure of non-freeness.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_BLOCKED at the freeness level.** Cébron Thm 2.13 cannot apply to Syracuse without an operator-valued / amalgamated-free generalization.

The natural "freeness escape" — **B-amalgamated freeness over a common subalgebra B ⊂ A** — exists in Voiculescu's later work (Voiculescu 1995, Speicher 1998 Memoirs AMS), but is **NOT in Cébron 2013** and **NOT in this corpus**.

### C-8. Large-N limit (§4.4 Theorem 4.6)

Cébron Thm 4.6: E tr P(G^{(N)}_t) = τ(P(G_t)) + O(1/N²).

Proof structure (p. 50-51):
> "Let d be the degree of P. Because the exponential map is differentiable, we have e^{(t/4)(Δ_{GL} + (1/N²) Δ̃_{GL})} = e^{(t/4) Δ_{GL}} + O(1/N²) in the finite dimensional space End(C^d{X, X*, X⁻¹, X*⁻¹})."

**Smooth-Lie use:** The convergence is driven by **differentiability of the exponential map on a finite-dim subspace of operators** — this is a finite-dim functional-calculus statement, and it would work for *any* family of operators (Δ_{GL}, Δ̃_{GL}) where the perturbation has appropriate matrix norm. **But the operators themselves come from smooth-Lie Laplacians.**

**Profinite analog:** Once one has the analog of Δ_{GL} and Δ̃_{GL} on the profinite side (which requires the construction of C-5 and C-6), the **finite-dim perturbation argument** is base-clean. But the **inputs** (the two Laplacians) are constructed via smooth calculus.

**Verdict: TRANSFER_TECHNICAL given C-5 and C-6, but C-5 and C-6 are TRANSFER_RESEARCH.** So the chain is **TRANSFER_RESEARCH**.

---

### C-9. Free Hall transform (§3.6, §4.3) — bonus check

The free Hall transform F_t maps L²(U_t, τ) to L²_hol(G_t, τ) — relates free unitary BM to free circular multiplicative BM. Uses Hall's classical theorem on GL_N(C) as the classical-finite-N analog.

**Smooth-Lie use:** Hall's theorem is a **smooth analytic continuation from U(N) to GL_N(C)** via the Laplacian. Hardcoded into the smooth structure.

**Profinite analog:** None obvious. The "analytic continuation U(N) → GL_N(C)" depends on complexification of a real Lie group; profinite groups have no obvious "complexification."

**Verdict: TRANSFER_RESEARCH** — but Hall transform is downstream of the C4 question, not load-bearing for Ch 11.

## The single most load-bearing profinite portability question

**Can Cébron's free-multiplicative-Brownian-motion (G_t) be replaced by a "free Brownian motion on Z_3*" (or on the inverse limit lim ← (Z/3^n)*), such that:**

1. **The composition formula τ(P(G_t)|B) = (e^{(t/4) Δ_{prof}} P)(B) holds** for an appropriate profinite analog of the derivation Δ_{GL}, AND
2. **The freeness hypothesis is relaxed to amalgamated-freeness over a subalgebra B ⊂ A that captures the 2-adic / 3-adic coupling**, AND
3. **The large-n limit (cyclic level → ∞) reproduces a finite-rate spectral statement** matching the PADE-observed radius 1/√3?

This is **three pieces of original mathematics**, the third of which depends on the first two. It is **research-monograph scope** — exactly the Ch 11 ceiling already identified in `PROFINITE_TRANSFER_OPERATOR_BLUEPRINT.md`.

---

## What Cébron DOES supply (positive findings)

1. **The multiplicative composition formula τ(P(AB)|B) = (e^{D_A} P)(B) is EXPLICIT and stated as a theorem (Thm 2.13)** — replacing the Tao-RMT punt to Speicher with a clean reference.

2. **The combinatorial machinery (free log-cumulants L_κ, graded Hopf algebra Y(k)) is base-clean and would transport to the profinite category at the algebra level.**

3. **The single-variable scalar form τ(P(A)) = (e^{D_A} P)(1)** (Thm 2.13 special case) gives a closed-form for moments of A — this could potentially be applied at FIXED level n to compute moments of the Syracuse one-step operator. But this is downstream of Ch 11.

These positive findings **sharpen the v1 status** (the multiplicative composition formula is now explicit and named) but do **NOT** unblock Ch 11.
