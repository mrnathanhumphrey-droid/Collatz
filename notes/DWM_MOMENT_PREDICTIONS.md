## 1. Setup

Per DWM identification:
- System state at step j: ρ_j(v_{1:j}) on ℋ_S — conditioned on observation history
- Centered operator at step j: X̃_j := T_j − φ(T_j) where φ = E_𝔅 is the conditional expectation onto the abelian observation algebra
- DWM expectation formula for an n-time moment:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2} ··· X̃_{j_n}) = E_v [ Tr( X̃_{j_n}^op ··· X̃_{j_1}^op · π_N ) ]

  where the outer E_v averages over the full Geom(1/2)^N observation trajectory and π_N = ρ_N(v_{1:N}) is the DWM-filtered state at the final step.

## 2. Row (b): cross-step second moment φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) for j_1 ≠ j_2

### Target: ~0 (noise floor 1.08×10⁻⁷). Verified Syracuse value.

### Audit-flagged hand-wave (Belavkin probe's argument)

The Belavkin probe (BELAVKIN_MOMENT_PREDICTIONS.md §2) claimed:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) = E[X̃_{j_1} · E[X̃_{j_2} | 𝔅_{j_2−1}]] = 0

via "tower property." The Belavkin audit's Finding 3.1 correctly identified that this requires `E[X̃_{j_2} | 𝔅_{j_2−1}] = 0` — i.e. X̃_j is a martingale difference relative to the filtration 𝔅_{j-1}, NOT just centered with respect to the full filtration 𝔅 = ⋃_k 𝔅_k.

### Honest DWM derivation

**Claim.** Under DWM with adaptive Kraus M_v^{(j, b_{[1,j-1]})} where the per-step v_j is independent of past b_{[1,j-1]} as a random variable (which holds for Syracuse because v_j ~ Geom(1/2) iid), the **conditional expectation E_𝔅[T_j | 𝔅_{j-1}]** factors as the projection onto the j-th step's Kraus channel applied to the past-state, integrated over v_j.

**Detailed step:**

Define T_j^{op} := T_j viewed as the operator on ℋ_n parametrized by b_{[1,j-1]}. The conditional expectation

  E[T_j | 𝔅_{j-1}] = ∑_{v ≥ 1} 2^{-v} · M_v^{(j, b_{[1,j-1]})}† M_v^{(j, b_{[1,j-1]})} = I

by the POVM resolution. So `E[T_j | 𝔅_{j-1}]` is **proportional to the identity** in 𝒜_S, with proportionality constant `Tr(I)/dim(ℋ_n) · ρ_{j-1}(b_{[1,j-1]})` in the tracial sense.

The full conditional expectation onto 𝔅 (the entire abelian observation algebra) of T_j is computed by averaging over **all future v_{j+1}, v_{j+2}, ...** as well. Under the Geom(1/2) iid structure, **the v_{j+1}, ..., v_N are independent of 𝔅_{j-1}**, so

  E[T_j | 𝔅] = E[T_j | 𝔅_∞] = same as E[T_j | 𝔅_{j-1}]   (since T_j has no v_k content for k > j, and the v_k for k > j contribute only through future operators not present in T_j).

Wait — this requires care. T_j has v_j content (via the Geom(1/2)-weighted sum in the Kraus channel) and b_{[1,j-1]} content (via the phase). Both are in 𝔅. So

  E_𝔅[T_j] = ∫ T_j(b_{[1,j-1]}, v_j) · 1_{b_{[1,j-1]} = b'} · 1_{v_j = v} · ...
          = T_j(b_{[1,j-1]}, v_j) itself, since T_j is already 𝔅-measurable up to its ℋ_n-operator content

— **but** the operator-content of T_j on ℋ_n is NOT 𝔅-measurable (𝔅 is the **classical** observation algebra, acting trivially on ℋ_n). So `E_𝔅[T_j]` is the **conditional expectation onto 𝔅 of T_j viewed as a B(ℋ_n ⊗ L²(Ω))-valued operator**, which reduces to averaging T_j(b, v_j) over the trace on ℋ_n at each (b, v_j) value.

So

  X̃_j = T_j − E_𝔅[T_j] = T_j − (Tr_{ℋ_n}[T_j(b, v_j)] / d_n) · I_{ℋ_n}

(centered tracially).

**Row (b) derivation:**

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) = E_𝔅[Tr_{ℋ_n}(X̃_{j_1}(b, v_{j_1}) X̃_{j_2}(b', v_{j_2}) · ρ_N)]

For the multi-time Kraus expectation, with j_1 < j_2 WLOG:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) = ∑_{v_1, ..., v_N} (∏ 2^{-v_k}) · Tr[ X̃_{j_2}^op · M^{(j_2,...)} ··· X̃_{j_1}^op · M^{(j_1, ...)} ··· ρ_0 ··· ]

This is **NOT obviously zero**. The vanishing must come from **the conditional independence structure** of the Geom(1/2) v's: averaging X̃_{j_2}(b, v_{j_2}) over v_{j_2} ~ Geom(1/2) gives — by definition of centering — the expectation over (b, v_{j_2}) traces is zero **WHEN one holds the past fixed via 𝔅-conditioning**.

**The key formal step (Mode-E gap closure):**

For the iid Geom(1/2) structure with the DWM Kraus form, the tower property holds onto **the σ-algebra generated by v_{1:j_2−1} via b_{[1,j_2−1]}**. Specifically:

  E[X̃_{j_2} | σ(v_{1:j_2−1})] = ∫_{v_{j_2}, v_{j_2+1}, ...} X̃_{j_2}(b_{[1,j_2-1]} + v_{j_2}, v_{j_2}) · 2^{-v_{j_2}} dν(v_{j_2+1:N})

But X̃_{j_2} is centered such that this integral vanishes at each fixed b_{[1,j_2-1]} — that's the definition of E_𝔅. **More carefully:** centering by E_𝔅 (the conditional expectation onto the FULL abelian past) gives `E_𝔅[X̃_{j_2}] = 0`. Since `σ(v_{1:j_2−1}) ⊂ 𝔅`, by the tower property

  E[X̃_{j_2} | σ(v_{1:j_2−1})] = E[ E_𝔅[X̃_{j_2}] | σ(v_{1:j_2−1}) ] = 0.

**This IS a clean martingale-difference statement.**

Then by tower again:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) = E[ X̃_{j_1} · E[X̃_{j_2} | σ(v_{1:j_2−1})] ] = E[ X̃_{j_1} · 0 ] = 0.   ✓

### Closure of audit Finding 3.1

The clean derivation requires:
(a) Geom(1/2) iid structure of v_1, v_2, ... ✓ Syracuse satisfies (definition of Tao recursion)
(b) Centering X̃_j = T_j − E_𝔅[T_j] with E_𝔅 = conditional expectation onto the FULL abelian filtration. ✓ Syracuse uses this centering (per `AMALG_FREENESS_SETUP.md` §5).
(c) σ(v_{1:j_2−1}) ⊆ 𝔅: ✓ (b_{[1,k]} for k ≤ j_2−1 is σ(v_{1:j_2−1})-measurable; the running-sum coarsening is on the side of "𝔅 is a sub-σ-algebra of σ(v_1, v_2, ...)", and the relevant σ(v_{1:j_2−1}) is even coarser — fine for the tower direction we need).

**Wait — the coarsening goes the wrong direction.** Let me re-check:

- σ(v_{1:j_2−1}) is the σ-algebra generated by the **individual** v's. This is **finer** than 𝔅_{j_2-1} = σ(b_{[1,1]}, ..., b_{[1,j_2−1]}).
- We have 𝔅 = σ(b_{[1,k]} : all k) ⊆ σ(v_1, v_2, ...) = σ(individual draws).
- For the tower from 𝔅 down to σ(v_{1:j_2−1}): does 𝔅 ⊆ σ(v_{1:j_2−1}) hold? NO — 𝔅 includes b_{[1,j_2]} = b_{[1,j_2−1]} + v_{j_2}, which is σ(v_{1:j_2})-measurable but not σ(v_{1:j_2−1})-measurable.

**Correct tower direction:** σ(v_{1:j_2−1}) is finer than 𝔅_{j_2−1} but not comparable to 𝔅 (since 𝔅 includes future-running-sums). To use `E_𝔅[X̃_{j_2}] = 0` for our row (b), we instead write:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) = E_𝔅[X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}]
                        = E_𝔅[X̃_{j_1} · E_𝔅[X̃_{j_2} | 𝔅_{j_2−1}, X̃_{j_1}]]   (no — this doesn't work cleanly because X̃_{j_1} ∉ 𝔅)

**The clean argument is operator-algebraic, not measure-theoretic on Ω alone.** It uses the Kraus channel structure directly:

For j_1 < j_2, expand using Kraus operators ordered in time:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) = ∑_{v_{1:N}} P(v_{1:N}) · Tr[ ρ_0 · (M_1)† ··· (M_{j_1-1})† X̃_{j_1}(b)† (M_{j_1})† ··· (M_{j_2-1})† X̃_{j_2}(b')† (M_{j_2})† ··· (M_N)† M_N ··· M_{j_1} X̃_{j_1}(b) M_{j_1-1} ··· M_1 ]

— wait this is for the trace of a product. Restructure as **Heisenberg-picture multi-time correlation**:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2}) = Tr[ Φ_{j_2,N} ∘ X̃_{j_2}^op ∘ Φ_{j_1,j_2} ∘ X̃_{j_1}^op ∘ Φ_{1,j_1} (ρ_0) ]

where Φ_{a,b} = ∏_{k=a}^{b-1} 𝒞_k is the composition of intermediate CP channels 𝒞_k(ρ) = ∑_v M_v^{(k)} ρ M_v^{(k)}†.

Now `X̃_{j_2}^op` is the **system-side operator** with E[X̃_{j_2}^op | 𝔅] = 0 averaged over v_{j_2}. Specifically:

  Tr[ Φ_{j_2,N}(X̃_{j_2}^op · Φ_{j_1,j_2}(X̃_{j_1}^op · Φ_{1,j_1}(ρ_0))) ]

For row (b) to be zero, the *outer trace against the X̃_{j_2}^op insertion* should vanish. Since X̃_{j_2}^op depends on (b_{[1,j_2−1]}, v_{j_2}) and is centered with E_𝔅[X̃_{j_2}^op] = 0 by definition, the **bath-averaged** Heisenberg observable

  X̃_{j_2}^{op, bath-avg} := ∑_{v_{j_2}} 2^{-v_{j_2}} · X̃_{j_2}(b_{[1,j_2-1]}, v_{j_2}) = 0   (per centering)

at each fixed b_{[1,j_2-1]} value. Combined with the structure that v_{j_2} is integrated out in Φ_{j_2-1, j_2}, the row (b) value reduces to the trace of an inner trace that vanishes pointwise.

**This is the structural reason for Syracuse's row (b) ≈ 0 under DWM** — and it requires the centering to use the FULL conditional expectation onto 𝔅 (as Syracuse does), not just 𝔅_{j_2-1}.

### Verdict on row (b)

**Predicted value:** 0 (exactly, modulo discretization at finite n).
**Match to Syracuse:** 0 ± 1.08×10⁻⁷. ✓

**Derivation status:** Cleaner than Belavkin probe's, but still relies on the bath-averaged-X̃ vanishing pointwise. The cleanest formal statement is: `X̃_j^op` has zero conditional expectation onto 𝔅, and the Heisenberg-picture trace factors through this conditional expectation when the j-th step is the innermost. **Audit Finding 3.1 is partially closed** — the iid-Geom + full-𝔅 centering structure is doing the work, but the precise operator-algebra factorization through bath-averaging is what makes it rigorous, not a naive tower property on σ-algebras only.

## 3. Row (d): third moment φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2} X̃_{j_1})

### Target: 0.108 (4 orders above noise). Verified Syracuse value.

### DWM derivation

Heisenberg form (j_1 < j_2):

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2} X̃_{j_1}) = Tr[ Φ_{j_2,N}(X̃_{j_2}^op · Φ_{j_1,j_2}(X̃_{j_1}^op · Φ_{1,j_1}(ρ_0))) · X̃_{j_1}^op ]

Wait — for the third moment with X̃_{j_1} appearing twice, the time-ordered product places X̃_{j_1} at positions 1 and 3. Reordering: in Heisenberg picture this is

  Tr[ Φ_{j_2,N}( X̃_{j_2}^op_R · Φ_{j_1,j_2}( X̃_{j_1}^op_L · Φ_{1,j_1}(ρ_0) ) · X̃_{j_2}^op_L ) · X̃_{j_1}^op_R ]

— or more cleanly, the n-time correlation function:

  φ(A_1(j_1) A_2(j_2) A_3(j_1)) = E_{traj}[ Tr( A_3 · M_{v_{j_1}}^{(j_1)} ··· M_{v_{j_2}}^{(j_2)} A_2 (M_{v_{j_2}}^{(j_2)})† ··· (M_{v_{j_1}}^{(j_1)})† A_1 ρ ... ) ]

The **two occurrences of X̃_{j_1}^op at different positions in the time-ordered product** sit on **opposite sides** of the inserted X̃_{j_2}^op. Unlike row (b) where the centering-to-zero argument cleared, here:

(i) The two X̃_{j_1}^op factors do NOT commute past X̃_{j_2}^op because intermediate Kraus operators M^{(j_2)} don't preserve eigenstructure of X̃_{j_1}^op.
(ii) The product X̃_{j_1}^op · M^{(j_2)} · X̃_{j_1}^op is a non-trivial operator on ℋ_n.
(iii) Its trace against the intermediate state is generically **non-zero**.

This is **why row (d) is non-zero** under DWM. The argument is operator-algebraic non-contraction, NOT a "framework property."

### Quantitative prediction

To predict the **specific value 0.108**, one needs the explicit Kraus-channel computation in R77's (1, 4) eigenbasis. The structural prediction is:

  φ(X̃_{j_1} X̃_{j_2} X̃_{j_1}) ≈ Tr( X̃_{j_1}^op · 𝒞_{j_2} · X̃_{j_1}^op · |1,4⟩⟨1,4| ) · (R77 magnitudes)

where `𝒞_{j_2}(·) = ∑_v M_v^{(j_2)} (·) (M_v^{(j_2)})†` is the j_2-step CP channel. **The numerical value 0.108 requires explicit numpy computation in the (1, 4) R77 basis** — this is the same Mode-E gap MP-G1 the Belavkin probe deferred.

### Verdict on row (d)

**Qualitative prediction:** non-zero. ✓ (matches Syracuse 0.108).
**Quantitative prediction:** requires Mode-E gap closure (explicit numpy Kraus-channel trace in R77 (1,4) basis); not closed this probe.

**Honest:** the qualitative "non-zero" prediction is **weak** (any non-degenerate Kraus framework predicts this). The genuine framework-discriminator is the **numerical magnitude 0.108**. Without that computation, "DWM admits row (d) non-zero" is essentially tautological. (This is the audit's Finding 3.2 critique, applied to DWM.)
