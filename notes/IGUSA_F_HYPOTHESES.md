# IGUSA_F — Bories-Cluckers polynomial mapping

## Phase 0 — verbatim

**Theorem 2.5 (Bories-Cluckers).** Let I be a monomial ideal in Z_p[x_1,…,x_n] and g a polynomial in Z_p[x_1,…,x_n], strongly non-degenerated over F_p with respect to Newton polyhedron Γ_g. Then:

Z_{I, g, dx}(s) = Σ_{δ ∈ Δ_{I,g}} L_δ · S_δ,

with L_δ = p^{-n}((p−1)^n − N_δ · (p+1)/p) and S_δ = Σ_{k ∈ Z^n_{≥0} ∩ δ} p^{−m_I(k)s − m_g(k) − σ(k)}.

The **real parts of candidate poles** are: s = −(m_g(k) + σ(k))/m_I(k) for k a primitive generator of a ray in Δ_{I,g}.

## Hypothesis types

- (i) **Polynomial mapping f = (f_1,…,f_t) or polynomial-ideal pair (I, g)**: I a monomial ideal; multi-component or with auxiliary g.
- (ii) Strongly non-degenerated over F_p.
- (iii) Newton polyhedron data.

## Phase 1 — substrate check

R78 substrate is a **single polynomial** g(u), not a polynomial mapping or polynomial-ideal pair. h_POLYNOMIAL_MAPPING_FORM FAILED.

Could artificially form a pair (I = (u), g = g(u))? I = (u) is the monomial ideal generated by u; Γ_I = ray [1,∞). Then Z_{(u), g, du}(s) = Σ_{k≥0} p^{-ks − v_3(g·u for ord(u)=k) − k} = ... but this is just a 1D version of the same formula.

Same as candidate B: in 1D the formula reduces to Denef-Hoornaert with the single facet at v=1, m(v)=1, σ(v)=1, giving pole at s = -1.

## Disposition: NO_FIT (inherits B's pole-location obstruction)

The Bories-Cluckers polynomial-mapping generalization does not produce new pole real-parts beyond {−(σ(v) + m_g(v))/m_I(v)} for primitive v. In 1D this is {-(1 + m_g(1))/1} ∈ {-1, -2, …}, still negative integers. log_3(2) inaccessible.
