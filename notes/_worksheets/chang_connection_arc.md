## Structural reframing

The trajectory measure on Z/32Z is **NOT a fixed quasi-stationary distribution**.
It is a one-parameter flow ρ_t(·) on the simplex over Z/32Z, indexed by
survival time t (equivalently, by survival fraction).

The drift is generated by integer-attractor absorption acting on Chang's
kernel P. As t grows, the survivor population thins: orbits that have
escaped many m_j absorbers are over-represented in increasingly extreme
tail residues. Result 22's σ-quartile Esscher tilt (long-σ ⟨v⟩ = 2.22),
Result 38's Gamma log-excursion shape, and Result 40's residue depletion
are all manifestations of the same flow at different observables.
