# FAURE candidate E — Faure 2008 "Semiclassical Anosov diffeomorphisms and Ruelle resonances"

**Source:** Faure 2008, extracted via pdfminer (pypdf custom-font garbled).

## Verbatim hypotheses (Theorem 1)

> "Let M be a **smooth compact connected manifold**. Let f: M → M be a **C^∞ diffeomorphism**... assume f is **Anosov**: there exists a φ_t-invariant splitting TM = E_u ⊕ E_s with exponential contraction/expansion."

> "**Definition 2.** Let L → M be a smooth complex line bundle over M. A transfer operator F̂ associated to the smooth diffeomorphism f: M → M is a linear map acting F̂: C^∞(M; L) → C^∞(M; L)."

> "**Theorem 1.** Let m be a function which satisfies the hypothesis of Lemma 2 [escape function on T*M]. Then F̂ extends as a bounded operator on the anisotropic Sobolev space H_m and has a discrete (Ruelle) spectrum [quasi-compact decomposition]."

## Identification

(i) THE MAP: C^∞ Anosov DIFFEOMORPHISM on a smooth compact manifold (not partially expanding — fully Anosov, hyperbolic on the whole tangent bundle).
(ii) FUNCTION SPACE: anisotropic Sobolev H_m with smooth order function m on T*M.
(iii) EXPANSION: uniform hyperbolicity on all of M.
(iv) CONCLUSION: discrete Ruelle spectrum, quasi-compact decomposition.

## Match to Syracuse

Syracuse has a NEUTRAL component (the 3-adic level shift μ̂_n → μ̂_{n+1} carries the chain forward without local expansion on Z_3) and an EXPANSION-LIKE component (the Geom(2) factor 2^{-a} acting via random multiplication). It is NOT fully Anosov — it's at most "partially expanding", which is why candidate A (Faure 2009) is the correct candidate to test. Faure 2008 (E) NO_FIT by hypothesis: Syracuse is not Anosov diffeo.
